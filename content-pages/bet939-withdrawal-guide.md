An active account
Sufficient withdrawable balance
Correct JazzCash, Easypaisa details
A verified mobile number

Completed bonus turnover, if applicable
A stable internet connection

Withdrawal Is Pending
A pending status means the request has been submitted but is still being reviewed or processed.
Common reasons include:
Account verification is incomplete
Payment details need checking
Bonus turnover is not complete
The payment channel is busy
The request is under a security review
A large amount needs manual approval
The account name does not match
Do not submit another request until the first one is completed or rejected.
Withdrawal Was Rejected
A request may be rejected because:
Incorrect wallet or bank details
Minimum withdrawal limit not met
Insufficient withdrawable balance
Incomplete account verification
Unfinished bonus conditions
Account name mismatch
Invalid payment method
Duplicate withdrawal request
Account restriction
Read the rejection message carefully before trying again.
Money Deducted but Not Received

First, check:
Withdrawal status
Request ID
Payment method
Account number
Submitted amount

If the transaction is marked as completed but the money has not arrived, contact the official support option and share the request ID.
Never share your password, OTP, wallet PIN, or banking password.
Wrong Wallet Number Entered
Contact official support as soon as possible if you entered the wrong number.
Correction may not be possible after the payment has been completed. Always check every digit before submitting a request.
Bonus Turnover Requirements

Expiry date
Maximum withdrawal
