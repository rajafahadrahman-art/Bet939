# How to Deposit Money to Your Bet939 Account

Depositing money to a Bet939 account is normally done through the wallet or cashier section. Pakistani users can add money through JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank transfer, 
The deposit process is simple, but users should check the receiver’s number, account name, minimum amount and payment instructions before sending money. Payment details can change, so do not use an old number saved in your phone or received from an unknown person.
Users who have not installed the app can first follow the Bet939 Game Download guide. Registered users can access their accounts through the Bet939 Game Login guide before starting the payment process.
What You Need Before Making a Deposit
Keep the following things ready:
An active account
A stable internet connection
Sufficient balance in JazzCash, Easypaisa or your bank account
Correct receiver details
Your user ID or registered mobile number
It is better to complete account registration and verification before making the first payment. Using your own mobile wallet or bank account can also reduce payment delays.
Payment Methods Available
Platform support local wallets in Pakistan to make app efficient and fast.
JazzCash
JazzCash is commonly used for mobile payments in Pakistan. Users can send money through the JazzCash.
Do not share your JazzCash PIN or OTP with anyone.
Easypaisa
Easypaisa is also available for adding funds. The process is similar to JazzCash, but users must follow the receiver details shown for Easypaisa.
Bank Transfer
Bank payments can take longer than mobile-wallet transactions. Keep the transaction reference number until the balance appears in your game wallet.
How to Deposit Money
Follow these general steps:
Open the Game App.
Open the Wallet (Deposit) section.
Select your preferred payment method.
Enter the amount you want to add.
Open JazzCash, Easypaisa, or your banking app.
You will receive an approval notification.
Approve the request.
Save the transaction ID or payment receipt.
Return to the app.
Check the deposit history until the balance is updated.
Do not press the submit button repeatedly. Multiple submissions can create confusion and delay the verification process.
Never send money to an Easypaisa number received through a random WhatsApp message.
Minimum and Maximum Deposit Limits
Minimum and maximum limits are different. The minimum limit is Rs.100 and Maximum is Rs.100000. Always check the amount displayed inside your account before making payment. 
A payment below the minimum amount may not be accepted automatically.
How Long Does a Deposit Take?
Wallet payments are usually received in 2 to 3 Minutes.
Processing time can depend on:
Selected payment method
Correct transaction details
Current payment traffic
Account verification
Payment amount
Network problems
Manual checking
Do not make another deposit just because the first one has not appeared immediately.
Deposit Is Pending
A pending status means the transaction has been submitted but is still being checked.
Common reasons include:
Payment confirmation is still in progress
Transaction ID was entered incorrectly
Payment was sent from another person’s account
Additional verification is required on wallet
Keep the payment receipt and wait for the shown processing period before contacting support.
Money Sent but Balance Not Updated
Check the deposit history first. If the payment does not appear, collect:
Transaction ID
Payment amount
Date and time
Screenshot of the completed transaction
Share this information only through the official support option available inside the app.
Never share your wallet PIN, banking password, or OTP.
Deposit Request Was Rejected
A deposit request may be rejected because:
Unsupported payment method
Duplicate request
Transaction was cancelled or failed
Read the rejection message carefully before submitting the details again.

Deposit Bonus

Maximum reward

Turnover requirement
Withdrawal conditions
